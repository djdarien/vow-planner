14. Technical Architecture

Vow Planner is built with a modular service layer, ensuring maintainability and scalability. The app uses a unified DataStore with atomic file writes and CloudKitSyncService for seamless offline-first functionality. This architecture allows users to plan weddings even in venues with poor reception.

While the app is highly efficient, users should note that the reliance on JSON persistence may limit scalability for large guest lists. Future updates may include migration to SwiftData for improved performance.

15. Product Design

The app features dual-role onboarding, allowing users to join as Hosts, Co-Planners, or Guests. The DashboardView provides an intuitive overview of wedding planning progress, while the SequentialGuestRSVPView simplifies the RSVP process for guests.

Vow Planner is designed to be a clean, fast, and private utility app, offering a native iOS experience without ads or unnecessary clutter.
